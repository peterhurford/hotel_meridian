    Hotel Meridian

    Midsummer Gala · 21 June 1929 · 8:00 PM to midnight

    You wake in Room 7 of the Hotel Meridian with no name in the register and a note on the nightstand, in your own handwriting: Stop the midnight toast.

    At midnight, the night ends badly, and the hotel rolls back to eight o'clock. You keep what you've learned. Nobody else does.

    The hotel has two faces. In the Sun it is gilded and alive: people see you, talk to you, and you can pick things up. In the Shadow it is the same hotel, rotted and broken: no one can see you, so you hear their private words and their thoughts. Some walls have crumbled there, and some halls are choked with rubble.
